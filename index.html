This Privacy Policy explains how IceCent Studio, a Discord bot developed by Icewalker, may collect, use, store, and protect information when people interact with it. The policy should be read alongside Discord's applicable terms and policies.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled and how the bot is configured, IceCent Studio may process:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs needed for bot features.
  • Commands and interactions: commands, button clicks, form submissions, or other interactions sent to the bot.
  • Message content: only where a feature requires it, such as processing a submitted message or moderation action.
  • Operational data: error reports or basic logs used to troubleshoot and maintain the bot.

The information actually processed depends on the bot's implementation and enabled features. Do not assume every category above is collected if the bot does not use it.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate requested bot features, respond to interactions, provide moderation or server-management functions where enabled, troubleshoot errors, prevent misuse, improve reliability, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and any hosting or integration providers required to run the bot. Information may also be disclosed with authorization or when required by applicable law. The bot should not send information to other parties except as described here or as clearly explained for a specific feature.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the bot's features, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. If IceCent Studio stores information, users may contact the developer to ask what is held and request deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained when required by law.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

IceCent Studio operates on Discord and may rely on hosting providers or integrations. Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirements and comply with applicable laws. IceCent Studio is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the bot or its data practices change. The latest version should be published at the same policy URL, and the effective date should be updated when appropriate.
